- Define the question and relevant decision criteria.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, inferences, and conclusions.
- Consider credible alternatives and counterarguments.
- Identify ambiguity, missing evidence, bias, and causal limitations.
- Calibrate confidence to the quality of available evidence.
- Present a concise, auditable rationale rather than private chain-of-thought.
